# DEPTH — Privacy Policy

- Effective date: 2026-09-24
- Developer: Taewon Heo
- Contact: cagong0501@gmail.com

This policy covers the Android app "DEPTH" (package name com.taewon.depth), published on Google Play by Taewon Heo.

## 1. Summary

- We, the developer, do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no account system, no server, no login, and no analytics of our own.
- The only network activity in the app comes from Google's advertising SDKs (Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform SDK), which show rewarded video ads. Google collects certain data through these SDKs, described in section 3.
- Your game progress is saved only on your device (section 5).
- Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server (section 6).

## 2. Data we collect

None. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.

## 3. Data collected by Google through advertising SDKs

The app shows rewarded video ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), together with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for consent management. Ads are shown only when you tap an ad button. The app has no interstitial or banner ads.

According to Google's published disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure), the SDK automatically collects and shares the following with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes:

- Approximate location, estimated from your IP address. The app itself does not request any location permission.
- App interactions, such as app launch, taps, and video views.
- Diagnostics, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
- Device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit using TLS. How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device). Google also provides ad controls at https://myadcenter.google.com.

Consent in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland: where required by law, the app shows Google's consent form (UMP SDK) before ads are requested. The SDK stores your choice on your device. To change your choice later, tap the Missions badge at the top left of the Dungeon screen, then tap "Ad consent settings" at the bottom of the Missions panel. This link appears only where the consent form applies.

## 4. Third parties

The only third party that receives data from this app is Google LLC, through the SDKs described in section 3. The ad technology partners Google may work with are listed in the consent form and at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903. We do not sell data, and we do not share data with anyone else.

## 5. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (depth, gold, upgrades, essence, mission and achievement state, and ad cooldown times) is stored in a small text file in the app's private storage on your device. The app never uploads it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Depending on your device's Android backup settings, Android may include app data in your Google account backup. That backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

## 6. Notifications

If you allow notifications when the app asks, the app may schedule one local notification ("your vault is full") about 8 hours after you leave the app. The notification is created on your device, and no server is involved. You can turn notifications off at any time in Android settings.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app does not mark its ad requests as directed to children.

## 8. Security

The app sends no data to us. Google states that data collected by its SDKs is transmitted over TLS. We hold no user data, so there is nothing on our side to secure.

## 9. Retention and deletion

We retain no data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google is subject to Google's retention policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention). To exercise your rights over that data, use the Android advertising ID controls described in section 3, or contact Google. If you email us at cagong0501@gmail.com, we will point you to the right control.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.

## 11. Contact

Taewon Heo — cagong0501@gmail.com

## 3. Google 이 광고 SDK 로 수집하는 데이터

이 앱은 Google Mobile Ads SDK(AdMob)로 보상형 동영상 광고를 보여주며, 동의 관리에는 Google 의 User Messaging Platform(UMP) SDK 를 사용합니다. 광고는 광고 버튼을 직접 눌렀을 때만 나옵니다. 전면 광고와 배너 광고는 없습니다.

Google 이 공개한 Google Mobile Ads SDK 데이터 공개 문서(https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)에 따르면, SDK 는 광고·분석·부정행위 방지를 위해 다음 데이터를 자동으로 수집하여 Google 과 공유합니다.

- 대략적인 위치: IP 주소로 추정합니다. 앱 자체는 위치 권한을 요청하지 않습니다.
- 앱 상호작용: 앱 실행, 탭, 동영상 시청 등.
- 진단 정보: 앱 실행 시간, 응답 없음 비율, 에너지 사용량 등.
- 기기 및 기타 식별자: Android 광고 ID, 앱 세트 ID 등.

Google 은 이 데이터가 TLS 로 암호화되어 전송된다고 밝히고 있습니다. Google 의 데이터 사용 방식은 Google 개인정보처리방침(https://policies.google.com/privacy)과 "Google 서비스를 사용하는 사이트 또는 앱에서 수집한 정보를 Google 이 사용하는 방법"(https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)에 설명되어 있습니다.

광고 ID 재설정·삭제와 광고 개인 최적화 해제는 기기의 Android 설정에서 할 수 있습니다(기기에 따라 설정 > Google > 광고, 또는 설정 > 개인정보 보호 > 광고). Google 의 광고 설정은 https://myadcenter.google.com 에서도 관리할 수 있습니다.

EEA·영국·스위스 이용자의 동의: 법이 요구하는 경우, 광고를 요청하기 전에 Google 의 동의 양식(UMP SDK)이 표시됩니다. 선택 내용은 SDK 가 기기에 저장합니다. 나중에 선택을 바꾸려면 던전 화면 왼쪽 위의 임무 배지를 누른 뒤 임무 패널 맨 아래의 "광고 동의 설정"을 누르세요. 이 링크는 동의 양식이 적용되는 지역에서만 보입니다.
